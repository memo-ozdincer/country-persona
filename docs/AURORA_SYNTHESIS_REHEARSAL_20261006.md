## Decision and limits

Keep the original parent, one-episode branch and new mixture. Working optimization, two learned full memos and retention of two exact controls are concrete wins. Dependable transfer is still missing, including a source reversal with a supplied correct plan. The correct direct care answer points to context/composition and scope as active weaknesses; it does not prove that a planner alone solves them.

Before increasing fitting data or dose, the next informative candidate is one small explicitly versioned native thinking/nonthinking comparison on the same exposed failed transfer source, with both weight arms and matching total output allowances. A short source-ledger realization comparison remains conditional if the failure persists. No next fit, protocol pack, queue or sweep is prepared.
