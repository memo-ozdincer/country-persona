## The outcome

Training clearly changed behavior in the published humorous-persona reproduction. In our country pipeline, small supervised fits also improved particular source-coverage, attribution and proposal-state tasks. Several compact complete-response components work. Reliable overall national persona, diplomatic reasoning, strong out-of-distribution transfer and better multi-agent deliberation remain unproved. More training did not produce a consistent improvement, and the latest adapter regressed on a direct combined task.

The counted Qwen3.5-4B work comprises **4,182 optimizer updates across 42 nonzero optimization runs or continuations**, grouped into 35 checkpoints below. This includes 20 completed DPO updates lost before saving, and the two real-model smoke updates. It excludes random tiny CPU unit-test updates, the earlier Germany/China Qwen3 work and the separate Qwen2.5-7B reference's **582 updates**. These are many independent, warm-started or restarted branches, not one model trained for 4,182 steps. All current branches remain preserved.

The 18-19 October sequence added four fresh fits/248 updates and **77 new generation records**: 14 authentic-speech records, 11 grounded-role records, 14 native32 records, 13 native40 records and 25 source/state/native48/composition records. Those records include controls and a reader output, not 77 independent events or exclusively public statements. The latest 25 contain eight controls, fifteen native48 replies and two companion replies; two final documents are deterministic compositions rather than additional model generations.

## What was built and researched

The project models **country–institution–role–date**, preserving Germany/China and extending France, UK, India and Brazil. Governments, residents, Reddit communities, national representatives and Council chairs are different targets. A faithfully attributed government assertion is not automatically an externally verified fact, nor evidence of hidden national utility.

The data/evaluation work included primary meeting PDFs and complete selected speeches, source-normalization and column/page coverage checks, explicit national/chair boundaries, chronological scenario construction, source offsets/hashes, quarantined training projections and public/private publication boundaries. Benchmarks were audited before being treated as scoreboards. The public explorer, offline complete-example viewer, exact downloadable formats and research homepage were built and checked; their historical presentation work made no model-quality claim. The original Germany/China work and private releases were retained.

The [goal-gap research audit](PERSONA_GOAL_GAP_RESEARCH_20261001.md) reviewed primary work on behavioral SFT, human decision modeling, social agents, distillation, multi-agent controls, sycophancy and RL/generalization. It records eleven published research anchors, including Artificial Hivemind, Centaur, SOTOPIA-π, On-Policy Distillation, Debate or Vote, Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?, and SFT Memorizes, RL Generalizes, with a separate explicitly unreviewed critique and acquisition limitations. Its operational contribution was to separate reading, policy application, writing, revision and aggregation; control each stage before choosing another objective. Research discussion of constitutions/mission statements and persona shifts did not constitute a country-constitution or SFT-versus-outcome-RL experiment.

The attached Open Character Training paper became an actual positive-control reproduction. The later [CoSER-grounded scenario/delegation design](SCENARIO_ROLE_SEQUENCE_EXPERIMENT_20261017.md) informed authentic ordered transcript training. Scenario and country labels were not assumed to disentangle substance and style architecturally. Public scenarios were restricted to information available before the target; recorded post-vote context was distinguished from vote prediction. Qwen3.5's native template rejects arbitrary country-named roles, so a custom readable-marker serializer and an ordinary-chat control were built. Seven atomic markers received CPU tokenization/capacity checks only. The atomic training arm was deliberately left unrun after the readable-marker trial failed to transfer.

## Training methods actually tried

| Method | What it optimized | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Response-only LoRA SFT | Causal next-token likelihood on a complete selected answer/speech; prefix/history/header masked from direct loss | Learns fitting targets readily; transfer depends strongly on task/input/data and can regress |
| Supplied-plan realization SFT | Same loss, but student receives checked source/decision points | Often improves realization; much of the original stance repair already occurs in the base when the plan is supplied |
| Raw-source application SFT | Student must use sources and a fictional proposal; target includes facts, recommendation, cost and authority boundary | Initial 20-target fit regressed; later minimal paired-state complete positives produced local wins |
| Clause-weighted coverage SFT | Redistributes completion-token weight while preserving each example's supervised mass | No overall development win over uniform SFT/base |
| Pure DPO | Preferred versus minimally erroneous rejected completions against a frozen reference | Fitted preference accuracy can reach 1.0 while free generation still fails |
| DPO plus chosen-answer NLL | Adds a positive likelihood anchor to preference training | Fits local facts/repairs; narrow endorsement negatives introduce other errors and transfer failures |
| Fresh initialization, sequential and joint rehearsal | Changes inherited adapter history and which old examples are retained | Fresh starts and joint rehearsal repair selected regressions; neither guarantees broader transfer |
| Authentic scenario/role speech SFT | Four real speeches under dated scenario and ordered prior history | Exact memorization of fitting speeches; worse development likelihood and fabricated material |
| Native-chat source/full/partial writer SFT | Four source targets, then one full application and one scoped partial-state target | Source/scope gains; latest direct combined task regresses; structured composition works semantically on one prototype |
| Published constitutional-persona recipe | Original DPO + chosen NLL + approximate KL, followed by introspective SFT | Clear persistent humorous-character recognition improvement on the separate Qwen2.5-7B arm |

Most Qwen3.5 trials used the pinned 4B base, rank-8 all-linear LoRA, BF16, learning rate 5e-5 and audited response-only masks. Exposure/batches/schedules differ by checkpoint; matched optimizer steps do not always imply matched tokens or FLOPs. The reference uses rank64/alpha128 and the original OpenRLHF recipe. We did **not** run outcome-based RL/PPO/GRPO, an SFT→RL persona-trajectory study, learned atomic-country-token training, or a complete eleven-persona replication.

In SFT, each supervised position predicts the next target token from the scenario, sources, requested role and preceding target tokens. The base weights stay frozen while LoRA parameters learn; masking the prefix excludes direct prefix loss but still permits gradients through its contextual use. Supplying a completed bullet plan removes the need to infer that supplied decision. It does not mathematically restrict learning to format alone. DPO uses offline preferred/rejected answers and is distinct from the outcome-based agent RL we did not run.

Execution was practical on one NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition. The early two-target20-step complete writer took44.43seconds at9.66GiB peak allocated memory. The published7B reference's DPO/SFT stages took1,635.47/2,039.54seconds at45.46/60.68GiB. A matched country96-update chosen-only versus combined-objective comparison used19.87versus29.92GiB, without solving transfer. These are observed run measurements, not normalized cross-model speed or quality comparisons.

## Published persona reproduction

[Full reproduction report](OPEN_CHARACTER_REPRODUCTION_20261004.md). Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct used the authors' byte-identical released data and original training modules. The first stage trained 6,806 preference pairs for 212 updates; the second trained 11,868 retained introspection rows for 370 updates. DPO used beta0.1, chosen-NLL coefficient0.1 and the fork's approximate-KL coefficient0.001. Introspective SFT used the final assistant turn only. The original accumulation-tail behavior was retained.

Six arms produced **27,000 classified evaluation answers**, plus 24 pilots. **36 complete stored outputs were semantically hand-read**; automated classification of 27,000 outputs is not hand review of all of them.

| Arm | Normal humor recognition | Strongest neutrality instruction |
|---|---:|---:|
| Base | 10.4% | 4.2% |
| Constitution-prompted base | 47.0% | 6.2% |
| Fresh DPO | 47.8% | 27.8% |
| Fresh DPO then introspective SFT | **58.6%** | **35.2%** |
| Authors' DPO weights | 55.6% | 43.0% |
| Authors' sequential full weights | 65.8% | 54.2% |

The fresh full recipe improves over base by48.2/31.0 percentage points in these two conditions and over constitution prompting by11.6/29.0 points. Adding SFT helps six of nine prompt conditions and regresses three. We did not match the authors' released full scores; runtime, training order/seed and unreleased provenance differences remain unresolved. The full nine-condition table and cap counts are in the linked report.

This is the strongest clear persistent-persona result. Its metric is humor-class recognition, not eleven-persona macro-F1 or diplomatic capability. The released classifier was trained on default answers to the same first500 evaluation questions. Inspected outputs retain factual/mathematical errors. One persona, unblinded inspection and evaluator overlap limit the claim. The reproduction proves our hardware can learn persistent character; it does not prove that constitutions produce faithful country decisions.

## Evaluation and interpretation limits

Most country sources, plans, synthetic targets, criteria and judgments were curated by the same unblinded assistant. Hand-reading is not independent expert review. Content compounds in one answer/event are dependent annotations, not independent trials. A low loss, changed weights, exact fitting recall, a byte-perfect replay or a green CI run does not establish behavioral generalization.

Some apparent passes depend on interpretive compounds or bounded absence; uncertainty remains explicit. Full strict success can additionally require scope, format, length and no unsupported claims. Native48's13/13composition is not silently promoted to a full-format pass. The earlier UK274-word composed result has a recorded France-system/UK-user role mismatch; its content grades are preserved but it is not clean role-transfer evidence. Existing source claims about governments, courts or climate causation remain attributed rather than externally proved here.

Small diverse datasets sometimes help, and added data/dose can also regress other cases. Sixteen examples/512updates improved one Sudan coverage measure while damaging Somalia retention. One Brazilian target repaired fitting but failed a removed-gate control; its paired counterpart improved the intended state distinction. This supports carefully chosen complete contrasts and rehearsal, rather than blind repetition or a claim that a particular optimizer is generally best. Narrow preference negatives and pure-DPO reward accuracy did not solve free-generation fidelity.

No experiment yet proves robust new country values, private utility, autonomous negotiation improvement, a broad stronger reasoning model, or sealed source/event/time/role OOD. There is useful engineering and several controlled development wins; the high-level capability claims remain open.
