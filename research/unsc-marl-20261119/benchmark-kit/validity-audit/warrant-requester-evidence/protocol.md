# Learn unknown partners from ordinary opening receipts

Freeze the three-delegate workshop and the full-information chair. Test the 12 existing held domains under both priors. Fixed opener conventions disclose a fitted slot, the highest local grade, or a zero grade when available. The mixed team combines these conventions by role. The coordinate-search opening tables come from the previously archived held experiment and represent an unfamiliar strategy outside the fitted style model. Rank every chair using the same partner and all evidence and link branches.

The style learner uses only the public domains and ordinary opening receipts from earlier episodes with the same team. It sees neither hidden cards nor subsequent queries and rewards. Compare a posterior over three homogeneous team models with a posterior over the 27 combinations of role-specific models. Both include ten percent support from independent uniform disclosures in each hypothesized model. Give both models the same prefixes of 0, 1, 4 and 16 previous episodes. Use one seed and 32 paired histories per condition. The 0-history, role-factored model is an important unlearned planner baseline. Also compare with the chair that ignores the opening convention, the chair assuming the fitted partner, and an oracle given the actual opening table.

Posterior inference may change which report the chair requests. It may never narrow the admissible world set in the publication certificate. The deployment net includes the same request fees and two-stage follow-up loss. Previous observations come from already completed workshop openings, with no extra calibration messages. They still have an observation delay and assume stable partners for calibration and deployment. The results measure receiver adaptation in typed multi-agent replay.
