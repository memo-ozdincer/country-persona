# Fit a less constrained disclosure likelihood

After seeing the role-factored model's residual regret on coordinate-search partners, hold all workshop rules and priors fixed. Reuse precisely the same 16-history draws. Test mixed and coordinate teams as the two unfamiliar cases. Fit a local categorical opening distribution for each delegate and each hypothetical card with 20 EM iterations. Infer unobserved card types from the known public prior, delivered grade values and chosen opening slots. Use two symmetric pseudocounts per local card type for the uniform estimator, and two pseudocounts distributed according to the three familiar conventions for the structured estimator. Evaluate requests under the learned opening likelihood, never use that likelihood in the decision certificate. This is a follow-up on exposed simulations with no new training of the opening actors.
