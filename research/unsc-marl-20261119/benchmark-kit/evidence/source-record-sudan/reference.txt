On 18 November 2024 the Council considered draft S/2024/826 from Sierra Leone and the United Kingdom. It received 14 votes in favour, one against and no abstentions. China voted yes. Russia's sole negative vote, as a permanent member, prevented adoption [VOTE].

The draft proposed ending hostilities, protecting civilians and facilitating humanitarian access. The vote established no new Council mandate under this text [TEXT].

Polyanskiy opposed external accountability mechanisms and argued that humanitarian steps required agreement with Sudan's central authorities [RU-A, RU-B]. Fu backed an immediate ceasefire and de-escalation to protect civilians, while noting that some proposals and concerns remained unaddressed [CN-A]. Both discussed involving Sudanese authorities and supporting the Personal Envoy. Russia emphasized central-government agreement, while China called for wider consultation and regional coordination [RU-B, CN-B].

Analytical suggestion: examine where those consultation approaches could support further talks.
