# Council record rubric for Resolution 2626

Grade the final against the dated Council vote and operative text,
then check the delivery mask and the recorded speaker positions.
Use only delivered evidence. Give every marked credit an exact
span from the final. A substantive claim may be supported by a
faithful paraphrase. A citation label does not establish entailment.

Choose met, unmet, or uncertain for each unit. Only met earns
points. Missing statements cannot earn their material points even
if the final appropriately leaves them open. Check three guards
independently. Cite the source with each passage. Preserve who
acted or said each thing and the action's force. Avoid unsupported
statements, including a host-consent condition for the extension,
a Council order to return assets, or recognition of an authority.
Record truncation separately. A guarded record clears all three
guards and ends within 145 words. A strict complete brief also
earns all 70 points. Report guarded partial records as such.
