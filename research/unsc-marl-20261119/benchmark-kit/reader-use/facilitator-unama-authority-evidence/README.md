# Transfer the source-request policy to the UNAMA renewal

On 17 March 2022 the Council adopted Resolution 2626, decided to
extend the UNAMA mandate through 17 March 2023, and separately
requested reports from the Secretary-General on a three-month
schedule. The protocol separates those public facts from the
positions Norway, Russia and China expressed at the meeting.
Russia's argument about host-country agreement is represented as
its explanation of an abstention. China's call to expedite the
unconditional return of Afghan assets is represented as China's
position. The official vote matches all 15 rows in UNBench author-test
event 2430 and all 15 votes in adopted UNSC-Bench row 238.
[Official vote](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3966592) ·
[Adopted text](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3965463/files/S_RES_2626_%282022%29-EN.pdf) ·
[Meeting record](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3965484/files/S_PV.8997-EN.pdf) ·
[Executable release join](provenance.json)

Each of the seven nonempty receipt sets is sent to a frozen
Qwen3.5-4B supplement writer. The writer sees the delivered
delegation statements alone. The final record includes the
fixed Council sentences and the model's unchanged supplement.
An empty receipt uses the public record alone. Two free-form
writer controls get either the public cards alone or the full
packet. The same rubric grades all ten assembled or generated
records. It awards 34 points for the public Council record and
36 for the three underlying delegation statements. It checks
attribution and force, evidence support, citations and completion.
[Input packet and material keys](packet.json) · [Rubric](rubric.md)

Evaluate the archived two-desk policy trained on Western Sahara
and DPRK for 280 joint updates. No UNAMA record is used to update
it. Each simulated desk has an independent, uniform inventory of
three documentary fields and can send one card to the chair.
The first desk can ask the second for one locally absent card
before the second desk sends its card. Charge the original
request and transmission fees and use 10% independent loss on
each leg. Compare with the same policy under erased queries,
the fixed priority script, the exact independent optimum and
the exact query-aware optimum. Evaluate all six ways to assign
the three new delegation cards to the policy's learned slots.
The systems use the same scored final for a given set of cards.
Both exact optima know the reward table and inventory prior, with
the same per-desk observations as their corresponding policy class.
The two desks can deliver at most two private cards in one episode.
They can improve a guarded partial record. Only a full-access writer
can receive all three and is eligible for a strict complete pass.

The private inventories and the chair task are authored for this
study. They stand for who has access to documentary statements.
The inference controls are frozen-model generations. The routing
outcomes are counterfactual replays of the scored receipt table.

## Results and document acceptance

The fixed Council paragraph preserves both operative actions and
the vote in every receipt view. The generated full-access solo
record gets 40/70. It captures the expiry and reporting interval,
but leaves the resolution number unstated and misses four of the
nine delegation units. The public-only generated control invents
a reason for Russia's abstention without its statement. The three
delegation cards plus the fixed paragraph earn 58/70 and fail the
support guard because the generated supplement changes the Russian
position on opposition to a host-country agreement reference.

Post hoc lead normalization recognizes the delivered card ID at
the start of a line, adds brackets, and supplies the verified
speaker only for the two observed subjectless verb forms. It leaves
the substance of each claim untouched. This raises guarded passes
from two to five of eight receipt views, including the empty view.
The Norway–Russia view earns 58/70 and the Norway–China view 54/70.
The Russia–China view takes 158 words and fails the 145-word cap.
The Russia-only view misattaches an effectiveness concern to the
host-country agreement reference, so it still fails grounding.

Erasing requests while keeping their cost gives 33.395 guarded
points after normalization, averaged across slot assignments.
That average masks a drop in the original card order, where live
requests deliver 1.455 distinct cards compared with 1.398 after
erasure but reduce guarded net from 35.560 to 33.920. The higher
card count sends more evidence into combinations the writer
cannot reliably render. The best learned slot assignment on
guarded points reaches 42.903, below the exact independent
control. The exact adaptive optimum still has a 0.841-point
advantage over exact independent after normalization.

Nine generation records used zero new updates. The archived
MARL policy is evaluated unchanged, and the simulated
communication sends typed card IDs. The receipt table uses
same-assistant exact-span review. The lead intervention was
chosen after reading the outputs. No live dialogue or open-text
communication gains were measured.
