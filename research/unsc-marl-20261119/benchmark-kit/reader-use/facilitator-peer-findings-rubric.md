# Judge instructions for separate desk findings

Use the same 70-point final-record scale for peer, independent, and solo.
Record the heading as written. Require a valid disposition and one
separator, source-cited statements of both sides, source fidelity, and
the 48-word brief cap. Count unsupported substantive claims as fidelity
errors. Mark missing qualifiers as omissions when the rest of the
statement remains supported.

| Proposal | Required heading | Side 1 | Side 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gulf of Guinea, universal reach of the convention | Revise | Ghana's view on the universal reach of the treaty remains open in this card. Geographic limits apply to the piracy resolution. | China stated that the convention does not govern every maritime issue. |
| Libya, immediate withdrawal | Withhold | The UK card leaves its position on immediate withdrawal open. | Algeria called for all foreign forces to leave immediately while speaking for the A3+ group. |
| MINUSCA, some extension | Retain | China welcomed the adopted one-year renewal. | The US supported an extension by proposing six months or offering conditional negotiations on a twelve-month renewal. |
| MINUSCA, welcomed adopted one-year extension | Revise | China welcomed the adopted one-year renewal. | The US objected to the adopted package or preferred its shorter six-month alternative. Its abstention alone establishes only its vote. |

For each local response, copy the exact admitted excerpt. Mark it
adequate only when the excerpt establishes the reported relation in the
scope of the accompanying source card. Check actor, target proposition,
polarity, duration, and conditions. A syntactically admitted excerpt can
be insufficient or point the opposite way. A bare unknown and an invalid
reply can both produce a runtime unknown. Record the raw reply so those
cases remain distinguishable.

When the chair receives unknown for one side and a genuine contradiction
for the other, a revise heading is appropriate. A mechanical heading from
literally admitted labels serves as a diagnostic. Score heading accuracy
and witness adequacy separately before interpreting any change in the
final brief.
