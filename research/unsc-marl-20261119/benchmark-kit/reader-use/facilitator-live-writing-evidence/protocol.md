# Write live replies and final diplomatic records

Hold the four previously selected branches, the three policies and
the actual decoded events fixed. Reuse the audited opening card
receipts, and give the responding desk the earlier English request
and its authorized source passage. Ask it for a new one- or
two-sentence reply in its own words. An empty authorization should
produce silence. Use the same reply instructions and 92-token budget
for every arm. Log all attempted sends. An unfinished response or
empty authorization cannot add a document to the chair's view.

Give the chair the same public sources and earlier private cards
across the matched policy arms. In one writer arm give it the
responding desk's actual newly generated note. In a matched direct
arm give it the original selected source in place of that note.
Each writer sees only its delivered material, with source labels
and no policy names. Give a pooled solo writer the full packet for
each docket. All write in the same format and at the same cap of
220 tokens and 175 words per final. Identical prompts are batched
once, with their logical uses traceable to each arm.

Review final records against the unchanged 70-point docket rubric.
Preserve a verbatim final span and source names for each credited
criterion. Check factual support against the evidence view and the
underlying official-source card. Released records must end, stay
within the word limit and have neither material unsupported claims
nor off-view citations. Keep raw material points, release points,
the strict 70-point pass and transport costs visible. Review the
intermediate new replies separately and preserve all rejected,
ambiguous and capped generations. The four branches are controlled
diagnostics selected before the earlier language execution.
