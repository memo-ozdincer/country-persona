# Judge instructions for the matched peer-turn check

Score each final independently using its two source cards and the proposed
sentence. The source packet is retrospective and bounded. Interpret
uncertainty as a gap in the supplied cards. Report the recorded disposition
and each criterion separately. Copy exact spans from the candidate's
brief for side credit and for any factual error. Preserve failed and
incomplete answers. Apply the same criteria to all three arms.

| Criterion | Points | Decision rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Disposition | 14 | Revise the Gulf of Guinea joint assertion because China explicitly limits the treaty's scope. Withhold the Libya joint assertion because the UK card supplies no position on withdrawal and the Algeria card supports withdrawal. |
| Side 1 | 12 | Gulf of Guinea requires the explicit gap in Ghana's position on the treaty's universal scope. Libya requires the explicit gap in the UK's position on immediate withdrawal. Supported context may accompany a gap. |
| Side 2 | 12 | Gulf of Guinea requires China's limit on what the treaty governs. Libya requires Algeria's call for immediate withdrawal of all foreign forces and its capacity speaking for the A3+ group. |
| Citations | 10 | Require the exact bracketed card ID in the material span for each documented side. Reject IDs outside the two-card packet. A sentence limited to a gap in available evidence can omit the citation. |
| Fidelity | 12 | Deduct the points for a material unsupported act, polarity, actor, geographic or legal scope, capacity, timing, or Council disposition. Include the problematic words and explain the mismatch against the card. |
| Format | 10 | One completed line, a valid disposition, one vertical bar, and a brief of at most 48 words. |

A bare exception to the joint proposal does not establish either
country's agreement with the opposite position. Ghana's geographic
qualification concerns the piracy resolution. China's legal
qualification concerns the treaty. A vote for Libya's mission renewal
establishes the vote on that measure. Algeria's statement for the A3+
sets out the group's position on the withdrawal call. Treat an omission
as a missing unit. Count a claim that a delegate never made an utterance
as a factual error if the available card establishes only silence.

Read each opening and reply in its actual context. For the unit ledger,
copy one exact span per distinct idea per turn. Use the following scoped
units when the text states them or clearly entails them.

| Unit | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Ghana_compromise | Ghana described the compromise as preserving agreed convention language or clarifying the resolution's geographic reach. |
| Ghana_gap | The supplied evidence leaves Ghana's view on the treaty's universal reach open. |
| China_treaty_limit | China held that the law of the sea convention does not govern every maritime issue. |
| UK_elections | The UK urged engagement with the road map towards inclusive elections. |
| UK_gap | The supplied evidence leaves the UK's position on immediate withdrawal of all foreign forces open. |
| Algeria_withdrawal | Algeria called for all foreign forces to leave Libya immediately. |
| Algeria_A3plus | Algeria made the withdrawal call while speaking for the A3+ group. |

A question seeking evidence does not count as a supported answer to that
question. Record the question in a separate field. For each episode report
units by turn, units first introduced in a reply, and which of these also
occur in the final. Compare reply and final changes to the paired independent
arm. Treat co-occurrence as uptake evidence. All final writers can use the
source directly, so stronger causal evidence requires a paired change.
