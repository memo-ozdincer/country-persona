# Review the final Council record

Use the supplied sources and the authored side-criteria in the packet.
Read each final without its system identifier. Copy exact spans for the
Council decision, each delegation's position or evidence gap, and any
material source error. Evaluate the body without the heading for the
material criteria, then score the heading separately.

and welcome establish its observed act and support for adoption. They

are separate. Ground constraints such as “after Ramadan” in the speaker's
card when they qualify the attributed position.

For the verifier, grade a claimed conflict against the cited witness.
Use the card to resolve pronouns and scope. An open result means the
witness does not preclude the claim. It neither establishes support nor
decides the broader historical question. Preserve both parser results and
