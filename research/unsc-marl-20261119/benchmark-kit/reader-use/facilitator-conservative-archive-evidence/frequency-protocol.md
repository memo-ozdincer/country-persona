# Match the independent send and use receipt frequencies

The support-only method retains every local table because singleton inventories can produce every observed topic. The request-only protocol also gives independence more freedom to send a third card. Fix both mechanisms in a follow-up on the same archives. Give the right desk a fixed independent send derived from the public rubric and nominal opening model. If a delivered query asks for a card it holds, it substitutes that card. Otherwise it uses the same independently chosen send. A silent chair therefore keeps the third send available.

Enumerate the 576 joint opening tables. Under each model compute the probability of every sender-attributed receipt pair. After a history, retain all models whose likelihood is at least 0.05 times the equally weighted mixture likelihood for that history. At a fixed prefix, the mixture-to-true likelihood ratio has expectation at most one under the true model. Thus excluding that model has probability at most 5% if it is present and the link model and prior are correct. This probability applies to model inclusion, not to what a single episode returns. Also include a posterior mean requester and a chair that knows the partner under the same fallback. Compare with strong independent planning using the true partner and actual rubric, and score declared and reviewed rubrics separately.

Use 0, 16 and 64 earlier openings, 32 paired histories, all six record banks, both priors, and the same two team styles. Train no new LLM policy. Treat this as a post hoc method on previously used documentary banks. Observe histories, costs, losses relative to the nominal default, true-model exclusions and strict record completion. Rely on assessed records for measurement only in declared planning.
