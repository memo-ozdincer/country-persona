# Fit the routing policy under reply rejection

Keep the two independent opening decisions fixed. The chair sees admitted opening receipts. The responder sees its own inventory, its intended opening send and the decoded request or silence. The third reply can be rejected for incomplete or unsupported evidence. Model reply admission by physical request and authorized reply topic. Charge every attempt before marking receipt. Pay the archived final-record score, a 21-point full-record bonus, and the existing message fees.

Train two seven-weight decentralized tails from the same initialization and documentary training rewards. One sees only perfect reply admission. The other sees four authored regimes with varying request-match, fallback and silence admission. Fit both for 240 shared-return updates. The robust policy is given only a declared admission estimate in its legal local action features. The exact public planner gets that same estimate and acts through the same information structure. Also report an exact planner with the measured or simulated admission table as an upper bound and the same opening. Keep actual admission out of policy choice.

Evaluate all inventory and link outcomes with uniform and correlated-duplicate inventories. Evaluate entire archived reward banks for UNIFIL, Myanmar, UNAMA and the emergency session under perfect and two authored noisy channels. Separately replay the measured Resolution 2593 matrix from its frozen generations and exact-span review. Its source-only writing cache yields a measured perfect-admission comparison. No held reward bank or measured admission matrix enters fitting. The noisy transfer banks are stress simulations. The Resolution 2593 endpoint is a replay with known generations and a fixed renderer. Evaluation uses a perfect query decoder conditional on physical delivery. Compare learned tails with initial action weights, independent delivery, public planning, and matched request erasure. Do not interpret simulator differences as improvements in free-form deliberation or language understanding.
