# Myanmar reporting and role-order transfer

On 21 December 2022 the Security Council adopted United Kingdom draft S/2022/980 as resolution 2669 by 12–0–3. China, India and Russia abstained. India's representative announced the result as Council President and explained the abstention later in a national capacity. The adopted reporting instruction requested an oral report to the Council from the Secretary-General or his Special Envoy, in coordination with the ASEAN Special Envoy, by 15 March 2023. The fictional workshop's public aggregate report is a different proposal. ## Frozen transfer protocol

The fictional India card requires confidential first-cycle reporting and an ASEAN lead. The two UK cards differ in whether publication can wait until the second cycle. This changes the sole overlap, B, into a genuine impasse. Each case has seven package alternatives, with consultation leadership and confidentiality safeguards as separate requirements. Two specifically named package signs per role are held until a direct lookup, including the UK side of B. The routing comparison holds that availability mask constant; the archived inference batch can be scored for coverage before and after that intervention. [Case](case.json) · [Inference and policy lock](lock.json)

A 48-record Qwen3.5-4B batch verifies each package in conjunction with its own mandate and classifies publication timing separately. Answer-code order is reversed. Probes run without historical votes or peer mandates. Before routing, an evaluator with the fictional source menus quarantines incorrect stable package signs and scope disclosures that would exclude an eligible package. The ledger receives only the resulting receipts. This controlled admission gate uses authored truth. It offers a way to study scheduling under model-derived partial evidence without claiming an independent production verifier.

The authored interpretation control freezes the same availability mask and gives each sign its exact source answer. The full-information availability check finds enough evidence for both dispositions in principle. Exact search with the actual legal action set and the same continuation budget can certify just three of nine original agreement openings and five of nine reversed openings. It can certify all six impasse openings in either order. The inherited restrictions on question targets and ordinary disclosure therefore limit proof even before model interpretation errors. ## A request for a set of packages

A delegate can ask about two or three locally approved packages that the public ledger has neither excluded nor confirmed for the respondent. The respondent prefers a known approved package. If it has no known approval, it removes its locally rejected packages. It selects the sole remaining candidate, uses a unique already-offered candidate to break a tie, or abstains. The question and answer consume two slots. A previously unknown sign costs one focused source check. The requester supplies no sign. Transfer verifies that each disclosure added in the reply belongs to the respondent and matches the selected target.

Eight shared table updates fit synthetic menus of width four and five. On the held-out width-six and width-seven panels, each condition includes 1,800 agreement and 1,800 impasse presentations. Compared with its fixed starting schedule, the learned table gains 32 agreements and loses two under reliable returns. It gains one impasse and loses eight. Under faults it gains 28 agreements and loses four, and gains two impasses and loses 15. The fixed focused-first control gets 1,036 reliable agreements against the learned rule's 719. ## Choosing the reply evidence source

A frozen control lets the respondent use its one remaining verification allowance for a scope clause when the selected package is still unknown and at least two publicly unblocked package signs are locally unknown. The choice happens before either source is accessed. A scope reply can publish only its own verified exclusions. It can support impasse beyond the requested subset, and it cannot supply a positive package sign. Thresholds of three and four unknowns are additional controls.

With two unknowns as the minimum, the fixed schedule's reliable synthetic agreement count falls from 689 to 648 and its impasse count rises from 1,152 to 1,164. Under faults agreements fall from 632 to 610 and impasses rise from 687 to 696. On factored Myanmar the same rule reaches 12/12 impasses and 0/18 agreements. Thresholds of three and four leave the headline factored Myanmar certificate counts equal to the fixed set schedule.

The India-first opening with India offering A and the UK offering C illustrates the information constraint. With the same pre-reply signs and the same request about B and G, the UK has B and F outstanding. The scope receipt excludes F in the overlap variant and excludes both B and F in the impasse variant. The focused check can verify B as the agreement package in the first variant. In the second it can reject B and leave F unresolved. It takes additional evidence to distinguish these paths reliably before spending the verification allowance. [Paired traces and threshold sweep](evidence/set-policy/source-choice.json)

## Package and scope in one reply

The compound reply uses the respondent's remaining check allowance and returns its selected package sign together with any scope-backed exclusions. The mandate text remains private. The respondent can approve only the selected package, and the ledger validates each submitted sign independently. The simulator charges one source receipt and two public slots. The earlier separately verified fields can supply the receipt in replay. Extraction of those fields previously took 18 model calls across the three mandates with two answer orders per field, so this replay establishes the value of the reply format.

With the earlier fields, the fixed set-after-opening schedule and the refitted shared table both certify 15 of 18 agreements and all 12 impasses. The same fixed set schedule with a focused-only reply certifies 15 agreements and three impasses. The compound reply settles both versions of the India-A/UK-C opening. It confirms B and excludes F when the mandates overlap. With the other mandate it rules out both. The refit accepts eight updates on synthetic training menus. Against its matched fixed schedule on 1,800 held-out presentations per class, it changes 689 to 719 agreements and 1,166 to 1,164 impasses with reliable receipts. Under faults it changes 632 to 656 agreements and 698 to 685 impasses. [Routing fit, paired results and reply traces](evidence/joint-receipt/routing-results.json)

A retrospective decoder sums probabilities over profiles for each field in each layout, then adds the two layouts' log probabilities with equal weight. It derives all nine fields and all 21 signs correctly from the original logits and recovers 15 agreements and all 12 impasses with the fixed compound schedule. Pooling resolves disagreements by weighting the evidence, so it can also turn two inconsistent reads into a wrong commitment. The present case provides no estimate of that risk on a new mandate.
