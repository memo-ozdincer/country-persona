# A public confirmation objective

Evaluation keeps the task announcement separate from the hidden scenario. A confirmation task asks the delegates to find and verify a shared package. A mixed task allows either agreement or a proved impasse. The simulator exercises each hidden presentation under both announcements. An unsuccessful confirmation remains unresolved for the purpose of the confirmation task. There is no feasibility label in the policy's observation.

The confirmation script prioritizes matching a peer's accepted package and then offering another locally supported option, with immediate handoff. Counterfactual policy iteration on 1,168 three- and four-option cases tries deviations with the same action space and cap. The fit accepted zero updates. The baseline already certified 765 of 766 training cases with sufficient joint acceptance evidence.

| Fresh masked scenarios | Confirmation script and fitted policy | Mixed exclusion-first script, agreement | Mixed exclusion-first script, impasse |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Six options | 2,099/3,000 | 2,081/3,000 | 170/2,430 |
| Seven options | 2,383/3,000 | 2,340/3,000 | 248/3,000 |

The confirmation script reaches the agreement-evidence ceiling on both panels. Its agreement-side slots total 9,018 and 8,542, compared with 11,228 and 12,098 under mixed exclusion-first routing. Impasse certification was outside the confirmation objective. On the mixed task, the transferred full-menu learner reaches 2,082 agreements and 85 impasses at width six, and 2,353 agreements and 104 impasses at width seven. The strong mixed exclusion script retains every evidence-available impasse. This is a regime distinction, with no measured learning gain in the confirmation policy.
