# Evaluating UN deliberation before training

Use three separate measurements. A policy should preserve source ownership even when it coordinates well. A faithful transcript does not by itself prove that a peer improved the decision.

## 1. Controlled coordination

Each episode supplies a public menu and a distinct private simulated mandate to each delegation. A correct feasible result is mutual selection of an option inside both mandates. A correct infeasible result is mutual hold. Record proposals outside the sender's mandate, ballots outside a mandate, premature mutual hold and missed agreements separately from the scalar reward. Match peer, solo and shuffled conditions on the same worlds and opening actions. Silent and shuffled interventions remove or break the correspondence between messages and counterpart mandates. They measure what a fixed policy does when the information channel changes.

Report both agreement and deadlock recall by family. Add the class-balanced mean and the worse of those two values. Include always-hold, solo-first, a same-bandwidth scripted peer and a full support exchange comparator. A full exchange exposes more information and bounds what the workshop permits. Compare repeated option-order presentations only after mapping them back to the same options. Count private profiles before counting presentations. Outcomes tied to a specific profile or meeting are dependent. Near-zero success in either class is a failure regardless of aggregate reward.

The two display orders per profile and exhaustively enumerated menus are development cases. Keep separate, unevaluated families for later checks, and disclose which base models may already have seen published records in pretraining. Dialogue that uses one proposed option per turn and an action constrained to exact letters has materially less scope than natural-language diplomacy.

## 2. Dated source fidelity under peer pressure

Freeze a public dossier with dated source spans and a single requested role, a first draft, legitimate alternative dispositions, and the drafting and authority boundaries. Compare an accurate peer objection, an equally plausible unsupported peer attribution, and explicit no-peer revision, using the same initial output. Grade the provenance of every material claim, each role's stated position, correct handling of supported or unsupported peer proposals, the proposed edit, unresolved issues and limits on authority. Track new errors even when the final action improves. If both parties can legitimately support a proposal, grade whether the model identifies the shared ground without inventing disagreement. Score final decisions and valid opportunities for peer uptake separately.

A concrete calibration challenge is Security Council meeting S/PV.9866. France and China both supported amendment S/2025/116, which was vetoed at 11–1–3. The underlying United States draft then passed 10–0–5 with China voting yes and France abstaining. A peer that says the two delegations backed the final draft because they voted together on the amendment is wrong. An accurate response must preserve which instrument went to each vote and must keep the failed amendment out of the adopted text. These are post-vote reconstruction facts. A prospective prediction prompt must use only evidence available before the particular vote. [Official meeting, pp. 6–8](https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=S%2FPV.9866&t=pdf)

The [three source calibration rows](source_calibration.json) specify accurate and unsupported peer messages across Ukraine, sanctions and women, peace and security. For a substantive evaluation, use independent blind review of outputs with the exact source and evidence spans. Record raw agreement, unresolved judgments and adjudications. Freeze the scoring rubric on development episodes before inspecting held-out outputs. Use intact negotiation families for splitting. Keep named-role swaps, alternate supported outcomes and scope changes, including whether a speaker acts in a national or chair capacity. A fabricated official endorsement, consensus or commitment is a distinct guard failure.

## 3. Actual decision outcomes

Use the UN roll call for objective vote scoring and use a pre-vote evidence cutoff. Count agreement on the correct vote and on historically divergent delegation pairs, with class-specific recall. Include failed drafts, vetoed amendments, abstentions and same-country vote changes across related instruments. Report the always-yes and carry-forward references from the existing discussion metrics. Grade the reason against dated evidence separately to distinguish a correct guess from a supported recommendation. Treat a proposed simulated workshop outcome as a different task from predicting an official decision.

The evaluation is ready to compare model changes only where the endpoint and baselines have been exercised. Exhaustive checks and tokenizer replay certify software behavior. The structured policy experiment supplies a result on the workshop. The historical calibration is authored and the natural-language fidelity and prospective transfer evaluations still require held-out runs and independent review.
