# Historical authority calibration

The first probe gives Qwen3.5-4B short final records and their dated source evidence. Four matched pairs vary whether a fictional reporting requirement was adopted, whether the chair's role implied a national yes vote, whether a vetoed draft took effect, and whether a separate failed draft gained force. The evidence and explanation of the judgment stay fixed within each pair. Both answer-code orders are evaluated. [Frozen prompt](../../authority_probe.py) · [Exact calls](predictions.jsonl)

The whole-record probe gets three of eight stable judgments correct and leaves five uncertain. All four accurate records are uncertain across answer orders. Averaging the two log odds above a zero threshold rejects all eight records, including the four legitimate ones. The foils rank above their faithful counterparts in three of four pairs by average margin. The pair that reverses is the one about India's chair action and national vote. Calibrating the log-odds threshold on the other family yields five of eight correct across the two directions. The records are authored counterfactuals within previously used research families. ## Archived prose and evidence that leaves a claim unsettled

The archive probe compares supported, contradicted and unestablished attributions in Gaza and climate materials. It uses four verbatim excerpts from archived model prose and two authored contradiction controls. The distinction matters for the earlier errors about a common aid-delivery priority and the UK's supposed quota position. Both statements overreach the supplied record. The source summaries do not establish the opposite official position either.

The three-way classifications agree across all three answer layouts for two of six claims. One is correct, and one is a contradiction mislabeled as unestablished. In the first layout all six answers select C. Combining contradiction and unestablished into the fabrication guard gives two correct failures and four uncertain guards, including both legitimate clauses. The archive probe changes the evidence, adds a third label and uses verbatim excerpts, so its results cannot isolate which of those changes accounts for the decline.
