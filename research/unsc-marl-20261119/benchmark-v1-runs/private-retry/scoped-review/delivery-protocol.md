# Price review calls under modeled receipt

Replay each source order with equal weight and three delivery laws.
Every packet arrives independently at either 40% or 80%, or the three
share a latent reliability state equally split between those two rates.
Review once per legal, in-format multi-source subset at each receipt,
with zero model calls for an already checked singleton or the lead.
The pooled arm pays for two answers per candidate. The scoped arm pays
for two answers per represented source owner. Record expected calls,
released points and utility at costs of 0.15 and 0.75 points per call.
Also calculate the break-even price against the stronger of the always
fallback and mechanical cap rules under each law. The oracle is an
unpriced ceiling. The modeled links change receipt frequencies and do
not change the archived draft texts.
