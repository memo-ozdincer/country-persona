# Learn who should ask for one documentary retry

Use exact released scores from the archived three-packet writer banks.
Treat each packet in turn as already available, giving two remaining
role packets and four observable initial receipt states. The empty
and singleton documentary scores are public. The two-packet score
is hidden from the policies. Freeze the latest composed Myanmar and
Resolution 2739 four-mask banks as primary transfer. Exclude all
Myanmar archive variants and Resolution 2739 from fitting. Give each
archive family equal weight over starter selections, writer variants
and both role orders. Keep writer failures and nonmonotone rewards.

A simulated link is good with first-send and retry probabilities
.8 and .9, or weak with .4 and .2. Each missing desk privately sees a
binary indicator of its link type. The accuracy is .85 in fitting
and in the matched replay, and .65 in the stressed replay. Cross
independent links with negatively correlated link types, with full
support over both types. The first delivery mask is public. Each
missing desk may send a one-bit hint at cost .15. Hint delivery is
assumed reliable. The chair sees the public receipt state and hints.
It can retry either missing packet at cost .75, or stop. A retry
that succeeds adds that packet to the actual archived writer mask.

Fit the shared desk head and chair head together for 200 exact
gradient updates using the terminal released points less modeled
message costs. Share parameters across roles and train from zero
with fixed penalty. Test leave-one-family-out on the archives and
both frozen transfer banks with no adaptation. Report greedy and
stochastic behavior. Match the learned agent's observations to fixed
policies using the receipt and posterior link probabilities. Compare
no hint, a good-signal hint and its inverse, with additive published
singleton coverage and a training-selected interaction offset.
Report the full score-bank and free-signal oracle as privileged
ceilings. Compute the strongest fixed result per cohort only as a
descriptive envelope, separate from training-selected deployment.
Keep fixed and fitted costs, released points and word-safe guards
from the archive visible. This is discrete team reinforcement with
observed documentary rewards and simulated channels.
