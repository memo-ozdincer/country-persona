Use two previously documented events that were absent from the original
2553/2721 team fit. A delegate may forward its received statement excerpt.
The document desk chooses between two pre-existing source claims. One
has greater standalone value. The other can earn a joint comparison
bonus if the appropriate delegate explanation arrives. Split the actual
evidence into roles and preserve all original item weights. Alternate
views omit parts of the original statement, with coverage scored under
the original compound rubric. These are experimental allocations.

Judge each source separately with the same frozen Qwen checkpoint,
role-scoped prompt, counterbalanced label mappings and positive mean
margin threshold. The native early judgment also supplies the reply
actor's received-message observation. Missing messages have a separate
observation. Preserve the native label even if it disagrees with the
authored key. Render fixed source statements and append the typed
comparison only if both native claims pass. Wrong added comparisons
receive zero under source audit. Freeze the authored keys before
reading outputs.

Initialize both actors from scratch, using the previous training
algorithms, seed, rates, prices and 512 updates. Compare the learned
delivery-value sender and standard joint REINFORCE at a matched
nominal reward-query budget. Benchmark a hindsight-optimized
arrival-only control and an oracle with the same native information
at deployment. Evaluate the 192 held rate, price and prior settings.
These two dockets are new to these fit routines and previously
exposed to this research project. Keep the v1.6 benchmark frozen.
