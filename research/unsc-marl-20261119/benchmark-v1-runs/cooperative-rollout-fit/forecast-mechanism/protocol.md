# Isolate the learned grade correction under exact routing

The prior comparison crossed a learned forecast and a different policy
construction. Here every arm uses the same exact adaptive planner,
openings, legal observations and cost. Transfer the frozen score model
on the same 126 conditions. Its strength stays at the value selected
on synthetic validation.

Before deployment vary only the two uncertain entries of the announced
bank. Cross the learned pair correction and learned full-receipt
correction in a two-by-two experiment. Include an upward-only version
of the pair correction. Include two nonlearned controls: move the pair
halfway toward its additive estimate and add a constant 3.5 points to
the pair. Each nonlearned control changes the pair only. Entry bounds,
all other observed scores and the inferred full score for pair-only
arms stay fixed. The constant is half the published stress offset.
None of the variants sees the true review offset.

Report held differences overall, by bank and by signed review offset.
Keep three outcomes separate: complete record coverage, net return
after communication, and quality below informed silence. Reconstruct
the true informed ceiling and replay every chosen policy independently.
No selection uses these exposed dossier scores. These are simulated
routing comparisons. They isolate one learned reward component.
