# Does the learner add a useful candidate under identical coordination?

Freeze this secondary synthetic test after observing the forecast fit.
Use the same paired holdout cases and both prior announcements. Compare
a portfolio of local-only, legacy fixed and refreshed fixed plans to
that same portfolio with each learned candidate added in turn. A common
chooser ranks all offered plans by expected documentary reward under the
announced inventory distribution. Keep the true inventory law out of
that decision. Break score ties by the declared candidate order.

Also include exact maximization under the announced law, and the
informed oracle under the actual law. Both receive the same typed payoff
table and forecast family as the portfolio chooser. Evaluate only on the
actual synthetic holdout law. This is a mechanism check with full
structured payoff terms, and is separate from the exposed archival
replay. It does not select a deployment policy or change the fit.
