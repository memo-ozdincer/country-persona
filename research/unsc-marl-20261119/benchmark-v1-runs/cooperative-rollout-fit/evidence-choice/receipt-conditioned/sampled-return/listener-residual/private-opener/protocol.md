# Learn after a partner has sent its only available clause

Earlier experiments trained three agents to select among public inventories, then evaluated transfer with a privately constrained opener. Here the first desk sends its only available clause during both training and evaluation. Compare a fair availability prior with a prior randomized across synthetic menus. Train both from uniform logits using the same menu features, seed, rewards, 150 updates and 96 rollouts per update. The listener sees the delivered clause when one arrives. The other deciding desk sees its own menu and receives the announced opening-card prior through the existing forecast features. Each update sees the realized terminal score for each rollout. It never asks for an unchosen branch's return.

Generate training and test menus independently with two clauses per desk, one send each, and a single randomly located relation for each pair of desks. Draw local points uniformly from 0–7 and relation points from 0–14. Draw a common receipt rate from the four existing rates. The varied arm draws the first-card prior uniformly from .25, .50 and .75. Hold 96 new menus per evaluation panel. The historical case actions, documents and scores are excluded from the training menus. The private availability is fictional in all cases.

Compare the two sampled fits, the previous public-inventory fit, the same-information fixed priority policy and the exact content and arrival ceilings. Check documentary transfer on both orders of Resolution 2553 and on Resolution 2422 with Russia first. All historical cases were previously exposed. Use exact dispatch to score the greedy policy. Report changes per family and the count of ceiling ties. Use final weights with no checkpoint selection.
