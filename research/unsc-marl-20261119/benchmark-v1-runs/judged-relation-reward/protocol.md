# Test a native relation grader as a sender reward

Use both complete reply records for each private documentary view in the
two-docket team environment. Supply the final brief, numbered authorized
sources and one existing relationship criterion. Ask a frozen Qwen3.5-4B
judge whether the particular relationship is explicit in the answer and
fully supported by the delivered sources. Run both decision-letter
mappings at the same generation budget, then average mapped logits and
use a zero cutoff. Do not expose oracle bits or points to the judge.

Also evaluate four claim substitutions, one per supported relation type.
These preserve surrounding facts while reversing timing support,
transferring Fund management to Belgium, treating consent as an operative
requirement, or replacing UNAMA. These mutations are diagnostic negatives
and do not enter sender training.

Keep factual standalone points fixed. In one replay replace only the
relation credit on the twelve actual complete records with the judge's
decision. Train the same reply-conditioned sender estimator on the same
128 randomized exchanges per docket and score it on the existing 1,152
held partner, delivery and price settings with the true archived reward.
Compare mean score bias, criterion recognition and decision regret
against the exact-reward fit. Report the guarded mutations separately
because they are outside the training outcome table.

This is a test of one native reward component and the consequences of
its errors. The renderer and exposed source families remain fixed.
No independent evaluator claim, full SFT, or released-benchmark
changes are part of this diagnostic.
