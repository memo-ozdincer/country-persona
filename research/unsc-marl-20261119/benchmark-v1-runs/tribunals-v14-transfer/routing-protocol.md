# Source-family policy transfer

Freeze separate interval teams and interval fixed-target controls from the
existing Myanmar and UNIFIL native receipt scores. Use the clean source
grades and keep the full-receipt grade out of policy selection. For each
source use the existing opening selector and each of the three advertised
mixtures or the corresponding dominant-only law. Freeze explicit opening,
chair and responder tables before reading the held model selections.

Apply these same tables to the Tribunals receipt bank. Then hold the same
opening tables and replan the chair and responder from seven target
grades under the announced law. The Peru–Russia pair is the only noisy
grade and is reported with -7, 0 and +7 point offsets. Leave the grade for
all three cards unseen by both replanned policies. An exact full-
information reference sees all eight grades and the true prior with the
same opening. Give fixed routing the same responder observations and the
same cache of selectable local packets as the team. Compare released
points, cost, query probability and net. Score the fully authored and
decoder-selected banks under the same series of channel assumptions.
When their score banks match, report the tied bank rather than duplicating
the number of conditions.

The main qualification compares frozen team versus frozen fixed, and
target-replanned team versus target-replanned fixed. Report wins, ties,
losses, mean and worst gains. Report margins to the strongest fully
informed fixed policy and quality loss versus exact silence separately.
The three mixtures and three review offsets are modeled settings on one
case. An opening is not refit on Tribunals. The original archive of the
Tribunals free-form writer remains a separate experiment with a different
score and budget.
