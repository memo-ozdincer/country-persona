# Select the best attainable quality when the exact floor is infeasible

The five-case transfer found 23 rows in which no adaptive chair map can
meet the documentary floor with a stochastic responder. The initial
fallback kept the original policy in those rows. Use the same draws,
four opening grades, seven continuation grades, scenario priors and
archived opening tables. Give the adaptive family the same fallback
criterion already applied to the restricted fixed family.

Among all legal chair maps and local responder distributions, maximize
the smallest forecast quality margin over informed silence. At that
quality, maximize the smallest net margin over informed fixed routing.
The selected policy can still have a negative forecast quality margin.
Do not change the policies in the other 67 rows. Compare the update
against the policy that was actually used before, the stronger of the
matched fixed controls, and fully informed silence. Report whether
the improvement in the forecast also improves held quality, as well as
all held costs and net changes.
