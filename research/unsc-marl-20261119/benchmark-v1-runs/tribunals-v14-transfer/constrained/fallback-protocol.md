Replay all 21 distinct Resolution 2422 settings. For each policy family
separately, retain the prior-envelope choice whenever its candidate pool
contains a policy meeting the documentary floor. On an empty qualification
pool, optimize the local randomized responder over the full legal chair
set for the family. Use the same priors, grades, openings, budgets and
held evaluation as before. If the fixed family still cannot meet the
floor, choose the policy with best worst forecast quality, using worst
net to break ties. If the adaptive family cannot attain the floor,
retain the archived result and record the unsuccessful search.

Report whether the original failure is repaired and whether the change
creates a new held loss below silence. Compare the old and updated team
against their respective updated or archived fixed family. Keep the
separate comparison against fully informed fixed routing. These are
distinct channel and scoring assumptions on the same one exposed case.
