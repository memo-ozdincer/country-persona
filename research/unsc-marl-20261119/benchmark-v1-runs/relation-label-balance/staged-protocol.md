# Fit the reviewer before pricing its decisions

Use the same 27 observed claim rows, four-family archive roster,
Myanmar transfer, option mappings and communication cost as the joint
fit. Keep the proposal actions, reviewer actions and information
boundaries identical. Every head receives 160 exact CPU updates with
the original step size and L2 coefficient. First update the reviewer
on both eligible query views equally. Its loss uses the expected
bound payoff of each legal reply, independent of the proposer's
current probability of contacting it. Then freeze its greedy
deployment and fit the proposer on the exact payoff of each reachable
final decision, with the additional read cost included.

Report greedy and expected sampling performance of the proposer with
the same frozen greedy reviewer. Reproduce the original joint fit and
controls. Include a component swap that keeps the joint proposer and
uses the staged reviewer, and one that keeps the staged proposer and
uses the joint reviewer. Test each archive family excluded from both
stages, plus Myanmar excluded from the full four-family fit. Report
all extra reads and whether they change a decision. The action bank
and costs were known before this intervention. This is exposed,
offline credit-assignment research with no model generation.
