# Compare correct workshop decisions with complete documentary reward

Use all eight published Iraq and Syria workshop worlds. Enumerate
literal receipt sets. A public chair sees the received mandates
and a predeclared set of possible local mandates, but not the
unreceived cards. Only recommend an option shared by all compatible
worlds, or record impasse if every compatible world lacks an option.
Use source-bound final rendering and the unchanged v1.7 mechanical,
authority, length and documentary criteria. Cite the published
rubric and known constraints in every final review.

Both desk orders use private mandates. Each desk can send its card
or withhold it. The second desk knows any delivered opening disclosure.
Both sends can be lost independently and charge the same cost for
attempting. Train exact shared-return policy gradients on either the
mechanical outcome or the released documentary score. Compare both
executed policies with scripted full disclosure, certificate-based
stopping and the exact decentralized optimum for each reward.
Certification reward values a supported correct decision at 70 points
for scale. Documentary reward uses the released rubric score out of 70.

Use a small fixed grid of rates and send prices. Each fit is for its
declared environment cell. Training does not estimate transfer to
unseen delivery laws or sources. The model is a finite-action relay
with deterministic source-grounded rendering, not an updated LLM.
