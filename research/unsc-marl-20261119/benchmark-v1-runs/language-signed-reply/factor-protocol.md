# Give mandate interpretation a separate training signal

The first reward fit eliminates many passes and creates four false
disclosures on held wording. Forcing the frozen logits to choose a sign
would be wrong on seven of 44 presentations, including four that could
certify a decision with the correct sign. Test a factorized head with
a compatibility estimate and a send-or-pass policy. Keep the same
examples, shared reward and 96-update budget. Apply an auxiliary
logistic loss with weight 0.25 for the compatibility estimate on all
synthetic prompts, including those on which passing is optimal. Compare
the same factorized action policy with its compatibility logits frozen.
Neither variant gets held facts or labels. Collect no new features.
