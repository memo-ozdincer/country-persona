# Fit source selection against a source-preserving tribunal chair

For each published one-block action pair and both presentation orders,
render the exact public vote, exact operative action, and the two
chosen excerpts. Carry the first sentence of the public limits card
verbatim. The second sentence concerns the separate ICTR early-release
discussion and is omitted uniformly. Apply the original word cap and
final-record rubric to all outputs. The editor produces no new factual
assertions. Grade the complete protected records with explicit cite
dependencies. Preserve the earlier native bank as the comparison.

Each desk privately receives a nonempty subset of its cards, using
independent retention at 65% and conditioning on nonempty sets.
Both desks choose one available card. Fit two decentralized categorical
policies by exact expected terminal reward gradient with both orders
equally weighted. Compare independent selection with an actor that can
condition its second decision on the first card. The learner gets
released rubric points, without direct supervision of source choices.
Evaluate greedy calls at retention rates of 45% and 85%, and report the
65% training rate for context. Compute exact independent and peer
optima, and a script using the published evidence weights alone.

This is a frozen case simulation with a deterministic renderer and
one authored rubric decomposition. The objective is to distinguish
capacity to use contextual messages from a gain over the known rule.
No natural-language model parameters change.
