# Learn from an already authorized last question

Use one ordinary final-slot decision from a uniformly selected
UNIFIL opening under the prior-only learned chair. Ask or stop as
the chair would normally do. All methods pay the same initial
dialogue costs and receive the same initial record. There is no
dedicated receipt request. Compare keeping the prior, updating
after a question whose role would send under every publicly
possible requirement, and updating after any question with the
public prior's predicted probability of sending. Also include a
naive update that treats every asked reply as mandatory.

The final send gate is the existing exact local gate. A receipt
requires that gate to permit sending, an available delegate, and
a successful known 0.80 channel. The evaluator uses the hidden
card only to determine gate behavior in simulated rollout. The
updater sees the asked role, public possible sign probabilities
and whether the reply was delivered. It uses receipt status
without reading the reply content. Enumerate all observation
branches, then replay the learned chair with updated mean
availability on future openings. Report the mean per future
dialogue. This adaptation uses simulated ordinary receipts in one
historical family and assumes the same partner persists.
