# Decide whom to probe and when to stop

Use the prior paid acknowledgment contract and four availability
profiles, with known 0.80 channel delivery. Permit up to two
acknowledgment requests per role. At each observed combination of
requests and receipts, choose a role to probe next or stop and
deploy with posterior mean availability. Compare the expected
future dialogue return with the acknowledgment cost. Charge the
send fee when an acknowledgment is sent, including erasure.

Fit the stopping and routing decisions using expected returns
replayed on the sixteen original synthetic workshop worlds.
Normalize field names without changing any set of compatible
options. The UNIFIL worlds and their returns stay out of probe
policy selection. For deployment lengths of 1, 5, 20 and 100
dialogues on a stable partner, evaluate the selected policy on
768 UNIFIL presentations per actual availability profile,
including the outside-prior profile. Compare no probes and the
previous fixed one or two per role. Keep individual profile results
alongside the prior-weighted mean. All receipt branches are
enumerated. No new Qwen calls, record annotations or language
training enter the comparison.
