# Infer response availability from paid acknowledgments

Keep the learned chair, local credit model, UNIFIL evaluation worlds,
and known 0.80 channel delivery. Before deliberating, each role can
be asked for up to two non-content acknowledgments. An available
role sends an acknowledgment irrespective of its private stance.
Absence is the only signal, and may come from erasure or unavailable
service. A question costs 0.04 and an acknowledgment costs 0.035
when sent. Update a uniform prior over the four declared availability
profiles using the observed counts. Deploy the learned policy
with the resulting posterior mean for each delegate's availability.

Use exact binomial probabilities over all receipt counts and
compare the prior-only policy, learned policy after one or two
probes per role, and known-availability reference. Add one actual
availability profile outside the prior's four support points.
The prior support, delivery model and hypothetical acknowledgment
contract are supplied. No live availability or private card labels
are supplied to the router. Report communication return per
opening and earliest fixed deployment horizon that benefits
from paying for the probes. A probe can amortize across a partner
that keeps the same availability. All results remain simulated.
