# Replay the fitted first responder with a less available last partner

Keep the two-question chair, fitted weights, first-link delivery
and each role's final signed preference fixed. After the last
question, allow Desk One or Desk Two to be unavailable, or let
both honor a productive request only 25% of the time. This
availability event is independent of channel erasure. The chair
pays for the question, and the responder pays its send cost
only when available and willing. Compare the frozen fitted
first policy with and without the declared partner availability
in its continuation features. The chair remains frozen and
can still ask the unavailable recipient. Compare with the same
public-payoff rule given the matching availability and with
mandatory first replies. There is no refitting in this stress
test. All behavior and co-player swaps are modeled.
