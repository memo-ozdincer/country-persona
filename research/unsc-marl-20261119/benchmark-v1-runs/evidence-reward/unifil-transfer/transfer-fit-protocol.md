# Fit the delivered record increment from synthetic public states

Use only the sixteen synthetic workshops in the original frozen
training roster. Generate all truthful opening subsets and all
one-sign queries in each. Represent the original public features
at a fixed delivery of one, with three additional Boolean
observations from the public candidate-count audit. These say
whether the addressed role or the other role is identifiable
before the reply and whether the verified sign would identify
the addressed role from its remaining compatible conditions.
The candidate count does not apply the sign to the decision
ledger or include the realized reward.

Fit a decision-tree regressor of maximum depth six on the delivered
record-score increment. Use the existing one-item attribution credit
and one-unit maximum, with no UNIFIL cases in fitting. At runtime
send only if the fitted increment times the supplied delivery
probability exceeds the fixed fee. Replay all UNIFIL presentations
at the original transfer rates. Compare the same complete record
proxy, disclosing any unsupported new observations or action errors.
This is supervised counterfactual credit on specified workshop
rules and a new case family. Final prose and natural-language
interaction remain outside this transfer test.
