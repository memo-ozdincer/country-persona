# Separate pessimistic channel inference from cautious action

Compare the correct likelihood with two decision changes on the same
24-workshop campaigns and random draws. The cautious rule selects a
positive expected-net team only if the current posterior puts at least
80 percent mass on that team's positive net. Otherwise choose silence.
The cutoff is fixed for the experiment.

The second rule permits one compulsory probe per role over a campaign.
Before a workshop, value each remaining probe by the next-reply expected
change to the best cautious policy's cumulative net over the remaining
workshops. The valuation holds each resulting posterior fixed for those
future workshops. Subtract the probe's question and reply fees. Buy at
most one probe per workshop, buying only the largest positive forecast.
Both cautious rules still learn from any ordinary workshop attempts.
Also replay the source-aware and nominal results under the same paired
draws for comparison. Physical delivery or future draws are unavailable
to the selector. This is an adaptive exploratory comparison on the
previous simulated case matrix, with the original estimators preserved.
