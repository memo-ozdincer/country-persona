# Choose between frozen teams using public expected return

The source-aware fit improves the six source-withheld net results but
regresses on several full-packet conditions. Test a choice among the
former fitted team, the source-aware team, and silence. Compare all
three at the start using exact evaluation under the public menu,
packet value, uniform requirement prior and supplied delivery rates.
Continue with the selected pair for the entire episode. Candidate
policy decisions and values may depend on private information only
through enumeration of the public prior. No actual hidden state
influences team selection. Preserve the exact adaptive planner as a
strong reference. This is public model-based selection of a learned
team and adds no fitting.

After confirming the matched case, hold the policy's believed rates at
the nominal profile. Replay the same decisions and selected team under
three shifted delivery laws. Report true net, silence and the hindsight
best candidate at true rates under the same deployed policies. Test a
uniform low estimate, the technical channel falling from the uniform
high profile, and the first role weakening under the old asymmetric
profile. These are fixed misspecification diagnostics.
