# Test coordinated changes across two public states

Start from the one-state policy search. At a local optimum, test all
legal moves that change two reachable public receipt states together,
reoptimizing the responder under every proposed map. If a move raises
team net, take it and return to single-state search. Continue to a
two-state local optimum. Reuse the same modeled reward and observation
kernel. Watch for changes that are only profitable together because they
change the mapping from heard signals to reply actions.

Evaluate on the sealed 120 inventory-and-case rows at the seven-grade
budget. Perturb the measured grade for pair 3 by minus seven, zero, or
plus seven before reconstructing the triple. Keep the other six grades
and all costs fixed. Compare single-state search, coordinated search,
and exhaustive adaptive planning on that same forecast. Evaluate each
against the strongest fixed policy with the actual bank and prior.
Track net margins, quality relative to optimized silence, queries and
the number of scored chair tables separately from the oracle audit.
