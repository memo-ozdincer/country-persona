# Isolate calibration and prior limits with an exact planner

Use the 120 zero-review-error rows of the sealed transfer matrix.
Keep the same opening policy, message costs, candidate observations and
advertised law. For the exact continuation planner, supply four grades
(empty and singletons), five (add pair 3), seven (add all pairs) or eight
(all masks). For the seven-grade view, predict the triple from the
sum of singleton terms plus measured pair residuals, with the same
documentary bounds. Score every policy on the actual held bank and law.
Compare to the true fixed and unrestricted controls. Full grades and
the true prior supply an upper bound for these fixed opening actions.
The full-grade rows assume unbiased review. Report by held bank suite and
prior information. This is an information diagnostic. Additional grades
are charged as review observations, separately from message costs.
