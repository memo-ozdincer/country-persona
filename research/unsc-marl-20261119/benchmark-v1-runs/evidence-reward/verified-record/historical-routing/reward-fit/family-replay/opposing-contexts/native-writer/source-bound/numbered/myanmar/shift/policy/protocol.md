# Improve a team policy from an optimized fixed request

Use the same 120 clean rows and the same four review budgets as the
previous information audit. Keep the opening policies, observation
boundaries, delivery law at evaluation, and message costs fixed. Build
a table of chair actions indexed only by the received evidence mask.
A response sees its own private inventory, its prior send, and the
question that reached it. For any chair table the responder can solve
its exact best reply under those observations.

Initialize the team with the best of three fixed targets and silence
on the advertised law and the calibration forecast. Test all legal
changes to the chair action at one reachable receipt mask. For each
proposal, fit the responder exactly to the new chair table. Accept
the greatest strictly improving team reward and repeat until no
single change improves it. Learn a new deterministic policy for each
case's forecast. Leave the language model and old shared heads frozen.
Preserve accepted moves, forecast reward, and how many chair tables
were evaluated. Use the exact exhaustive forecast solution as a
planning ceiling, with the held fixed and adaptive optima as controls.
Score actual net and released quality on the sealed true bank and law.
The policy update uses enumerated modeled rewards and no held-bank
feedback.
