# Preserve the expected quality of the no-request final record

The mixed selector increases estimated net in the emergency-session
archive while reducing expected released points. Apply a public
quality floor in a separate replay. Admit a candidate only if its
expected documentary score, computed from the empty and singleton
records, is at least the estimated no-request documentary score.
Within that set choose best predicted net, preferring no-request
on ties. Measure both quantities against the true archived banks.
This rule uses no observed pair or triple scores. The replay tests
a quality-prioritizing policy on the same exposed cases.
