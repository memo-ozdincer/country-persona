# Distinguish a received card from a complete position

Freeze the original seven-grade Somalia policies for all 18 prior
and review conditions. In a separate modeled stress test, vary the
probability that an otherwise successful, addressed U.S. reply
contains only the yes vote and conditions-review statement. That
partial reply remains admissible and carries the same communication
cost. It earns neither of the criteria requiring the reporting
objection. Under the frozen rubric its grade equals the same record
with the U.S. bit removed. The probability applies only to received
U.S. replies addressed to the U.S. and spans 0, .10, .25, .50 and
1.00. Openings and all other messages retain their full rubric
coverage. Compare equally informed fixed and coordinated routing.
These rates are assumptions for sensitivity analysis. A selected
native branch gives evidence that omission can occur.
