# Distinguish the evidence-target policy across three public states

With the same private inventory, a fixed ask for a card in the
symmetric difference of two receipt masks can reveal which mask
occurred. The recipient can act on that information even when
replying with a different card. Compare three distinct public states
from the same two opening sends and inventory. In this comparison
a legal fixed ask or silence has at most two distinct observations.

Keep the prior and control setup of the matched-pair comparison.
Search both two- and three-state settings. Report the two-state
result as a negative control. For a live candidate require a frozen
learned route that uses at least two different nonzero targets and
beats the strongest pair- or triplet-optimized fixed responder.
