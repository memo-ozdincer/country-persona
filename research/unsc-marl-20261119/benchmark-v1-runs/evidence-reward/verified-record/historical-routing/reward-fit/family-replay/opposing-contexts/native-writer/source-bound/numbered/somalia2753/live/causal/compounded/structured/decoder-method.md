# Evaluate content after constraining the ID format

Greedy prefix masking chooses among legal next tokens. The probability
of a valid prefix's complete descendants can differ even when the
prefixes have similar immediate scores. The experiment therefore
checks released documentary points, chosen passages, and logical token
cost along with wire validity. Park et al. study the related distortion
in sampling under grammar constraints. Their method considers the
probability of valid futures. The finite canonical menu here uses
ordinary greedy prefix masking. [Grammar-Aligned Decoding](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/2bdc2267c3d7d01523e2e17ac0a754f3-Abstract-Conference.html)

The chair and responder lookup tables are a finite partial-observation
control problem. The earlier policy-search comparisons estimate the
value of table changes before actor realization. The structured-output
experiment checks whether those planned messages reach the record.
Decentralized policy iteration is relevant prior work for the routing
part. [Policy Iteration for Decentralized Control of Markov Decision Processes](https://ocs.aaai.org/Papers/JAIR/Vol34/JAIR-3404.pdf)
