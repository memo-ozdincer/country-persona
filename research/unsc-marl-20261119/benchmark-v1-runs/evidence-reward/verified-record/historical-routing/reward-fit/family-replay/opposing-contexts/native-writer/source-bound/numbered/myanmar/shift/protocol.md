# Test the frozen router outside its calibration cases and priors

Use eight previously held dense banks and eight held sparse banks,
picked by a stable hash of the integer score vector. Exclude any bank
appearing in either routing fit. Keep the four historical docket banks
as a separately reported exposed suite. Substitute the two numbered
writer banks where they equal the existing bank. Record which outcomes
have real documentary grades and which come from the synthetic model.

Fix pair mask 3 throughout. Historical UNIFIL and Myanmar use their
cross-role criterion, while the additive cases serve as controls.
The synthetic pair is chosen by slot order and independently of the
hidden bank. Hold opening actions fixed using the advertised prior
and singleton scores. Compare three new convex mixtures of the three
known inventory laws. Test both an honestly supplied mixture and a
shift where the policy is told the dominant known component. Each cell
uses review errors of minus seven, zero and plus seven. Keep costs and
the delivery model unchanged. Give the fixed and silent references
the true law and true score bank. Report results by suite, whether
the prior was correct and the sign of the review error. This is an
exact-enumeration replay with frozen weights.
