Use the exposed UNIFIL renewal record, frozen dense and sparse weights,
and the quality-floor selector with only the empty and singleton scores.
Take the first existing inventory law under which that selector chooses a
learned team. Hold the original two opening policies fixed. Enumerate
private inventory pairs in numeric order and select the first pair where,
under otherwise ideal signed delivery, the learned team asks a question
and its public receipt value exceeds both no-request and request erasure.

population effect.

Use four arms. Keep the chosen learned pair, erase its query but charge
the attempt, compare an optimized no-request policy, and compare the best
legal fixed request on the four-score forecast. All have identical
held inventories, opening replies, compiler and no transport loss except
the deliberate erasure. The quote-producing delegates see their one
selected source, their role, and the public question only at the reply
stage. Admit only the full exact source block associated with their assigned
card and a valid response envelope. If a reply fails, use the resulting
public mask. Deduplicate exact prompts, with no parser tuning after the
run. Freeze a cap of six distinct native calls. Score the compiled
historical text with the original UNIFIL rubric. Preserve both final
documentary quality and modeled costs. This is a selected live receipt
exercise with a source-checked authored renderer and modeled possessions.
