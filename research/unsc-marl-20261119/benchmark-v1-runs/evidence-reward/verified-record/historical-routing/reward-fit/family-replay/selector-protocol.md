# Select a continuation from four calibration scores

Use the empty and three singleton records to estimate the score surface.
With this estimated surface and the fixed openers, build controls and
both learned-pruned continuations. Select the highest predicted net
from the learned fits and a no-request policy. In a second arm also
allow all three addressed fixed requests, with the optimized reply
policy on the same estimated surface. Prefer no-request on a tie.
Measure the selected policy only after selection against the full
archived record scores. Include the actual-score adaptive and
no-request bounds, and score how much held-record quality was lost
or gained separately from communication cost.

This is an adaptive composition of frozen teams developed after
the archived replay. The input restriction governs each policy
decision. It does not make the exposed historical cases blind.
