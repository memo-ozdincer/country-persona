# Qualify coordinated routing under uncertain pair reviews

Use the same 360 seven-grade conditions, with six clean calibration
grades and an uncertain pair-3 grade. At decision time consider the
observed grade and one criterion on either side. Form a pool containing
the coordinated policy and four fixed-or-silent policies optimized
under each forecast. Select on the advertised prior. Require held
candidate forecasts to meet each scenario's optimized silence
documentary value. Among eligible policies, maximize the worst net
margin relative to the strongest fixed forecast. If the floor is
infeasible, minimize the largest shortfall before optimizing net.
Evaluate three methods on the sealed actual bank and law. The nominal
method takes the coordinated policy at the reported grade. The
interval fixed control uses the same qualification rule with only
fixed and silent policies. The interval team can use both. This
compares adaptability at equal review and prior information.
