Keep the archived opening maps for Resolution 2422. Reserve the two actual
2422 score banks entirely for evaluation. Draw 144 synthetic record reward
banks using dated-case-inspired additive claims, paired claims and a
supplied word-budget ceiling. Vary the relative value of the three evidence
cards and the complementarity of their contents. Fit a chair and reply head
jointly using the expected team reward under the three existing modeled
inventory laws and the existing transport and fees. Use raw released
points minus costs with no strict-completion bonus.

inventories. The responder sees its local holdings, its own earlier send
and the delivered request. The published chair strategy permits a posterior
over public masks given that local observation. Use this posterior for
reply features in training and hard-policy evaluation. Neither actor sees
the other agent's inventory. Save one fixed-seed, 120-update fit.

its exact responder, exact no request, and exact joint continuation on
the same raw objective. Swap the fitted chair with an exact responder
to identify shortfalls from question choice. Also evaluate a separate
synthetic draw after fitting without using it for tuning. The actual case
