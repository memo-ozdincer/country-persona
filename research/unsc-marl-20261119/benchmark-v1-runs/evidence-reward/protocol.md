# Reward decision proofs and evidence for both mandates

Use the same 44 Iraq and Syria prompt presentations as the signed
reply comparison. Keep their public openings, designated package
question and 0.8 delivery rate. Compare silence, the existing
one-package sign to its designated recipient and asking either role
for its complete mandate. One question and one possible reply are
allowed. Compose full replies from the matching role's independently
archived declaration. This is a counterfactual delivery replay.

The chair models exactly one condition per role chosen uniformly
among conditions compatible with the visible signs. The public ledger
requires explicit signs for a terminal proof. Also count whether
each role's full constraint is identifiable within that model.
A complete record opportunity needs both an explicit proof and both
constraints identifiable. Inference from prior sign claims remains
distinct from verbatim mandate disclosure.

Score a decision proof at one point, with a predeclared 0.5 extra
for a complete record opportunity in the evidence-aware arm. Models
pay 0.04 per question, 0.035 per sign and 0.10 per complete reply,
even on loss. A responder may pass based on its own requirement and
the public state. First compare exact policies under decision-only
reward and evidence-aware reward, on the same available actions.
Compare the exact evidence-aware policy limited to the original sign
with the two expanded arms. Keep actual proof, sufficiency and cost
metrics beside scalar return. This bounds what learning can improve
under these communication and delivery assumptions.
