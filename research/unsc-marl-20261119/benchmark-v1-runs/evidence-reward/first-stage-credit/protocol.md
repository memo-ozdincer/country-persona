# Train the first signed reply against the final record

Use the original frozen Qwen3.5-4B feature cache and the original
144 synthetic and 44 Iraq and Syria presentations. The latter
remain fit-excluded. Use the same 96 CPU updates, initial logits,
linear head, optimizer and regularizer for both reward arms. The
decision arm must reproduce the earlier feature head's held actions.
The documentary arm values supported decisions at released coverage
out of 70 with the cost of any later mandate request. Use the public
14-point attribution weight. Under the synthetic model, a record
with a proof and unidentified mandate earns 56/70. A profitable
later request can recover the item with independent delivery. Keep
the original false-sign penalty for fitting. The no-send action may
retain an existing decision. The designated recipient and sign
question remain fixed.

For evaluation compare frozen, decision-reward, documentary-reward,
and exact locally verified controls. Report raw false replies,
proof-capable signed replies and reach into the four observed live
follow-up contexts. Score expected final coverage and cost after
rejecting invalid signs. For held cases, use the frozen reviewed
final texts for every reachable truthful branch. Score the known
replies at the original 0.8 delivery in both stages. Record whether
the changed reward changes the exact best action or only its margin.
No fresh model generation or Qwen parameter updates are used.
