# Learn when to send the locally verified sign

The matched record-reward fit sends eight additional truthful
replies with no extra released points. Keep the saved Qwen features,
the synthetic and held splits, and the role-local typed verification.
Use the verified local sign to restrict the actor to that sign or pass.
For each synthetic row, fit a binary residual on the difference of the
original sign and pass logits. The target is the positive signed
counterfactual value of sending under the record reward. Balance send
and pass in the logistic loss. Use the original 96 CPU update budget,
learning rate and residual regularization. Keep held outcomes outside
fitting.

Compare the binary head with masking illegal signs only at decoding
for each archived head, and with the exact locally verified rule.
Measure useful proofs, reachable live follow-ups, cleanly withheld
replies, released record points and the same costs. Local verification
depends on the trusted typed role card. This test changes how the
model decides to communicate without optimizing its representation of
the card or the first question.
