# Stress the learned responder under costly first-link erasure

Freeze the ledger-only head and the four admitted live follow-up
replies. Vary delivery of the first sign at 0.02, 0.04, 0.08 and
0.80, with follow-up delivery held at 0.80. Recompute each sign's
expected scored record and communication cost from the same verified
delivered and erased branches. Keep the action set fixed as verified
sign or pass and assess every held prompt. Compare the learned
decision to the exact cost-aware action at each rate. The lower
rates test whether adding current proof state also calibrates the
cost of communicating on an unreliable link.
