# Route among three roles on the Mali workshop

The two recorded Council votes are historical source material.
Three fictional workshop desks each privately accept one of two
values for their designated field. A package needs compatibility
with all three. A sign about package A reveals only the addressed
role's field, whose possible values and mapping are public. Infer
other compatibilities from that declared rule. Proof can end early
when known requirements exclude every package. Eight equally
likely worlds cover both outcomes. Credit a certified record at
0.8 when a role's condition is still unreported and 1.0 when all
three are reported, with no credit for a still-unresolved decision.
Charge 0.075 per question including the reply, independent of delivery.

Train a regression tree on exact public action values from
synthetic four-package menus with three binary fields. Exclude the
Mali menu and every menu isomorphic under field permutations and
value flips. Compare a fixed role order, one-step greedy, learned
lookahead router and exact public router. Hold the training channels
distinct from the evaluation channels. Run all eight worlds at
budgets one, two and three. Keep evaluation conditional on public
signs, keep delivery independent, and distinguish final disposition,
attribution credit and cost. This first evaluation uses the same
documentary proxy as the earlier typed UNIFIL test.
