# Learn a chair and a reply policy from sampled team return

Keep the Mali case and public certificate rule. Train on four-option menus
whose shapes exclude all role-permuted and bit-flipped versions of Mali.
Each episode samples an independent uniform hidden world and a public
delivery profile. The chair can stop or query one unsigned role. A queried
delegate can withhold or send its truthful sign. Charge 0.075 per query and
0.03 per send attempt, including lost attempts. Silence and transmission
failure have the same public appearance and neither certifies a requirement.
Score terminal documentary credit with the existing public certificate rule.
The cost is new and is applied to all policies in this comparison.

Use two actor networks. The chair scores candidate queries using their public
delivery and how their possible signs would partition the menu. A parameter-
shared reply actor reads the same public information plus its own requirement
and computes features for the one sign it can truthfully send. No actor
observes the other roles' hidden requirements. Learn both with sampled team
return and leave-one-out baselines grouped by public progress. No training
labels or planned action choices. Use one seed and one locked budget. Compare
the fitted pair with its initialization, component swaps, fixed order and
the exact planner with both optional and compulsory sending. Evaluate the
Mali menu under the previously used delivery profiles and budgets two and
three. Preserve terminal accuracy and distinguish agreement and impasse.
