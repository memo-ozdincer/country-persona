# Price and minimize access to retrospective receipt history

Use the exact same two hidden partners and priors as crossplay.py.
Offer up to four reads of independently drawn previous logged turns.
A read reveals the local observation and either chair delivery
presence, the first report's package only, its numeric grade only,
or the full first report. The entire read costs 0.001, 0.003 or
0.010 normalized reward points. This is a conditional cost study,
rather than an empirical processing-cost estimate.

At each history, choose whether to stop and plan the next decision
or pay for one further read. Compute the optimum for the declared
candidate partner pair by exact recursion. Compare equal-prior
planning without reads, fixed decks of one and four reads, and
the known-partner bound. Replay actual accesses separately by
partner as a mathematical check. The current turn and its final
record use the same original evidence and actions in every arm.
