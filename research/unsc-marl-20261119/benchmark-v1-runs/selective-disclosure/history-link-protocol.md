# Distinguish a changed world mix from a degraded peer link

Keep the six grids, original fee, frozen opener and chair verifier.
Use two unobserved world mixtures and two unobserved peer delivery
rates, 0.8 and 0.4, crossing these into four equally likely regimes.
A history plus the next decision share one regime. Freeze the opener's
actual decisions at its original nominal calibration. The recipient's
history is its own card and the delivered peer grade or silence.
Compare exact passive-history replay at 0, 4 and 16 turns.

Keep the fixed forecast and learned recipient actions from the
nominal link as deployment controls. Compare posterior action
planning with the nominal link held fixed against planning that
jointly estimates the two rates and two priors. Both see the
same observation history and choose from the same three recipient
actions. Use a declared-regime fixed-opener ceiling. Score each
actual regime separately and report the equal-weight mean.
This is a controlled model-misspecification test outside the frozen
release. Those two candidate link rates and world mixtures are
given to the joint planner, rather than discovered.
