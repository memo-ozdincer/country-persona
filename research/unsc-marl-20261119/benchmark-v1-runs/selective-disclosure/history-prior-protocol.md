# Infer the world mixture from ordinary recipient history

Freeze both previously evaluated recipient policies and the selective
opener. Compare balanced and uniform workshop worlds. The hidden
regime is held constant for an entire history plus the evaluated turn
and is equally likely before seeing any history. The recipient knows
the two candidate public distributions, possible grade cards and
the opener's public rule. It does not know which regime generated
the history. There are no history labels, rewards, or extra probe calls.

Enumerate the exact probability of each observed history via its
likelihood ratio under the two candidate regimes. Compare the previous
recipient's own cards alone with its own cards plus the peer grades
that arrived. Silence includes either withheld or erased messages.
Replay 0, 4, 16 and 32 past turns. Run the four reserved positive grids
and both source-linked transfer grids under both hidden regimes.

Compare a fixed forecast, unconditional ranking, a switch to the
ranking policy when the posterior favors balanced worlds, and an
exact public-model decision based on the inferred posterior and the
current observation. Also compute the most valuable policy from the
fixed/ranking pair under that posterior and a separate ceiling when
the true regime is declared. Budget, fees and the chair's logical
warrant stay fixed. No historical observation or posterior inference
is submitted as grade evidence. This is a stationary synthetic history
test on exposed grade grids with perfect model specification.
