screened before the original two-actor fit. Freeze the original first
desk's marginal distribution over packages and the second desk's
whole policy, including its response to a delivered grade. For each
first desk's private card and possible package, fit the incremental
team value of adding a peer message. Targets enumerate all compatible
worlds and transport events in fitting grids. Fit by weighted linear
least squares with no held targets. The final chair and legal evidence
grammar remain fixed.

cards, its fitted correction, the original joint policy, always pay,
never pay, and an exact gate that uses the declared deployment prior.

public grade grid. Neither receives the other desk's actual private
card. The exact gate bounds any send-only intervention retaining the
frozen package choices. The original exact joint two-desk policy
allows a separate package change and serves as a wider ceiling.
