# Learn when to forward a scope judgment

Two desks review the same candidate claim and its registered witness
under opposite option mappings. The first desk can send its actionable
local label or abstain. The second desk can approve or veto that sent
label given its own local logits. The policy may not change the label.
The first desk sees only its own logits. The second desk sees its own
logits and the received categorical label, without peer logits or keys.

Use the 19 routed, historically scored witness cases from the
archived relation test on UNIFIL, Colombia, Haiti and Mali. Exclude its
eight authored controls. Weight families equally and evaluate both
desk orders. Optimize exact team return over send and approve actions
for 160 CPU updates from zero at step size .2 and L2 penalty .02.
Correct actionable admission earns +1, wrong admission −2, withholding
zero, and a sent claim incurs a .05 point message charge. The proposer
features are an intercept, bounded local top margin and contradiction
indicator. The reviewer features are an intercept, bounded local
margin for the received label, local agreement indicator and received
contradiction indicator. An unresolved proposer may only withhold.
Compare both the greedy and the stochastic fitted policies without
retuning. Include first desk alone, unanimous, no-opposing-actionable,
silent and a pooled mean-margin reference. The pooled reference sees
both mappings at once. It pays the same message charge on admission.

Cross-validate by holding out one archival family at a time. Also train
on all four families and freeze before collecting eight new 2739
claims in two option mappings. The new claims contain grounded,
contradicted and unresolved cases involving reporting, recorded votes
and sponsor scope. Local gold labels follow the archived case source
packs and remain outside the model requests and policy features.
Compare the two learned gate components under the same action set.
Report admissions, unsupported admissions, cost, decision accuracy and
net utility, then identify which observation collisions drive losses.
This is a cooperative evidence gate with modeled payoff and measured
Qwen logits. It uses the previous source-bound chair as motivation.
It does not measure whole-record performance or train language-model
weights. The public 2739 case has prior project exposure.
