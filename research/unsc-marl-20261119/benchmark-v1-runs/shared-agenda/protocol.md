# Coordinate two workshop decisions with one report slot per desk

The two public docket menus come from the frozen Iraq and Syria workshop
tracks. A desk represents its role on both dockets. It can report one
available authenticated fictional mandate, or wait. Each chair chooses
only a decision that is acceptable in every frozen workshop world still
consistent with its admitted cards. Docket weights, transport success
rates and distributions of both desks' card availability are public.
The acting desk observes its own availability only. Availability,
relative importance, and transport are modeled. No diplomatic private
information is inferred.

Compare simultaneous reports and a sequential channel in which the second
desk sees whether and where the first report reached the chair. It sees
the docket ID but does not use the mandate contents when choosing an
action. Each single report costs 0.06 points. Correct warranted workshop
decisions earn the docket's public weight. Withholding earns zero. The
certificate enumerator computes whether a single card is sufficient
on each workshop world. Verify all 16 full-agenda pairs and all
receipt combinations. Uniform workshop worlds are independent of
the modeled inventory and link law. The reporting policies see
inventory and docket priorities, without mandate contents. The
chair sees the authenticated cards and uses the actual world
completion sets. Report the two regimes separately and preserve the
frozen benchmark's original evaluation contract.

Train two local linear softmax policies, one at each desk, for 300 full
batch CPU Adam updates of size .03 from zeros per channel. Use exact
expected shared return with L2 strength .001. One deterministic
generated training panel varies availability priors, link rates and
docket weights over 36 settings. Freeze each fitted policy before the
held panel of 48 conditions. The latter uses separate draws, rate ranges,
and a different correlation mechanism. The chair's certificate is fixed.
No model generation or judge calls enter the reward.

Evaluate greedy deployment and sampled policies. Exact enumeration of
all legal deterministic inventory policies gives the same-information
ceiling, with or without receipt feedback. Fixed controls include
optimized shared priority or a single target docket, a local expected
value rule using public probabilities and private availability, always
waiting and a fixed truthful follow-on after a receipt. Swap one learned
seat with the respective strongest simple fixed controls. Perform
an all-probability and branch-return check and an independently
enumerated deterministic replay. Report signed deltas and win counts
rather than interpreting a training objective as a holdout result.
