After observing the receipt-only fit, preserve all workshop worlds,
availability laws, priorities, message fees and link rates. Split each
receipt observed by the second desk into decision already warranted and
second card needed. The observation is computed by the exact public
certificate checker. The first acting desk still sees the same
availability and public prior. It remains blind to the particular
mandates. The two report choices and wait remain legal regardless
of evidence state.

Recompute the expected outcome and jointly refit the same two linear
softmax heads with one additional flag and the same initialization,
optimizer and budget. Compare with the frozen receipt-only heads
receiving an ordinary receipt under either proof status. Compare with
the fixed proof-aware follower and its exact same-view first actor.
Test a component swap that retains the learned opener but runs the fixed
follower. Report the number and cost of redundant reports and the
result on all 48 unchanged held scenarios. No training or action
labels from the holdout enter the fit.
