training-fee policy. Change only the second desk's action when the
public proof flag is true and it has the relevant second card. Work with
the observed eight final records for each writer, not forecasts for
conditions where no writer call was collected.

the docket's public weight times the frozen rubric's released-point
difference divided by 70. Charge the report fee even when it is erased.

of a delivered second card from exact team returns on one workshop
family. Apply it on the other without a refit, then reverse the family
split. Fits for different writer pipelines are separate. Use the mean
over both family directions and the 48 held availability scenarios.
Uniform worlds and prior setup retain the agenda assumptions.

independent outcome enumeration. This is a documentary marginal-value
replay on two exposed dockets under simulated delivery.
