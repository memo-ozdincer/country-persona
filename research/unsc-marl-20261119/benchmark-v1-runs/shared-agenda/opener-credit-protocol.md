# Learn the opening value after holding the proof-aware follower fixed

On the existing 36 fitting scenarios, enumerate the realized team
returns for each feasible opener action under the exact proof-aware
follower. Fit a linear action-value estimate to these returns using
the probability of the opener's own inventory as the row weight.
Compare the original opener features with a public accounting of its
standalone decision value, incremental decision value from a possible
reply, immediate fee and expected follow-up fee. The latter uses the
announced conditional probability of an eligible follower and its
published response rule. No private follower inventory enters the
opener. The accounting supplies feature quantities, with coefficient
signs and sizes learned from the reward.

Replay the unchanged 48 held scenarios at the .06 training fee and at
.015, .15 and .25 without a refit. The follower uses the same known
best response at every fee. Compare the frozen jointly fitted opener,
the two action-value fits, a fixed rule that prices only the opening
report and the exact same-information planner. Verify branch outcomes
separately from learned action values. Report fit residuals and which
held results are explained by the public accounting.
