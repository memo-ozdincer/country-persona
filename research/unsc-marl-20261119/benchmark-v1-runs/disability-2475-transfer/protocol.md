# Prospective Resolution 2475 source-pair test

This case evaluates the protection of persons with disabilities in
armed conflict under Resolution 2475. Its issue family has no prior
experimental result in this project. UNBench lists the vote in its
author-test release. The pinned UNSC-Bench adopted records have no
matching instrument or meeting. The case source blocks paraphrase the
official English meeting record and adopted resolution.

Freeze a ten-item, 70-point rubric before inference. Two criteria
test relations between France and Russia, and between the UK and
Russia. France supports broad implementation of the disability
convention, whereas Russia reads an operative reference narrowly.
The UK agrees with Russia about avoiding new legal obligations;
this does not imply UK agreement with Russia's Article 11 reading.
The materials that establish each relation are assigned to different
delegates. The maximal documentary scores for these authored relations
hold authority and source attribution fixed.

Generate three source-local notes and one public lead using frozen
Qwen3.5-4B. The renderer reuses these exact fragments in each of eight
receipt sets. Compare that verbatim assembly with a single additional
relation sentence generated from the notes actually received. Make
one comparison call each for masks 3, 6 and 7, where a keyed relation
is documentable. The other masks use the same exact answer in both
arms. The comparison writer sees no source blocks, rubric, constructed
answer, hidden notes or outcome scores. There are seven physical calls,
no retries, and no text edits. The official v1.5 release stays fixed.

Primary readouts are exact guarded release across eight masks, and the
scores for each joint criterion. Compare measured pair scores to the
additive estimate from the same empty and singleton records. A margin
can come from new synthesis, loss of valid content, or a guard/length
failure. Trace any margin to a specific criterion. The live experiment
does not assume that the chair has already acquired the notes under a
learned policy.
