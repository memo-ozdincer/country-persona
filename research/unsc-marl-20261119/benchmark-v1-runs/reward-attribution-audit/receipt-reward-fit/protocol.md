# Learn undisclosed relationship values from realized final scores

Freeze the verified source-rendered reward tables on Resolutions 2422 and 2553. On each docket, collect simulated exploratory exchanges with equal random choices for the later and third desks. The earlier desk can forward its one available clause. Its first option has probability .25, and independent delivery on all links is .55. Each learner receives per-exchange feedback consisting of the selected source IDs that actually arrived and the single terminal released score. It knows the public and standalone credits. It does not know which relationship is scored, the scores of unchosen branches, nonarriving source IDs, or the event's relationship definitions.

Compare two reward learners using the same data. The record lookup estimates missing credit separately for each complete receipt tuple, with the known standalone sum as its fallback. The pair fit subtracts known public and single-source credit, then fits a ridge solution over all 12 possible cross-role combinations equally, with penalty .5 and no event-specific flags. Use either fitted reward table to coordinate the later desk's receipt-dependent choice and the third desk's fixed choice. The planner sees its learned reward table and the announced priors and delivery. The learned pair table is available at planning time after training. Compare to standalone, an optimally coordinated static policy fitted to the same learned bank, and a full-information ceiling.

The training design uses one fixed seed to generate 256 paired histories per docket, with 4, 16, 64 and 256 observation prefixes. Exclude the collection condition from exact deployment replay. Include both Resolution 2553 desk orders, treating the order change as an independently learned receipt environment. Evaluate exact net-free score out of 70 and probability of reaching the ceiling across repeated simulated histories. Verify the learned predictor against independent dispatch and track empirical reward-table error separately. All dockets and scorers are exposed; this measures within-docket reward acquisition and transfer across transport conditions. There are no native calls or language-model updates.
