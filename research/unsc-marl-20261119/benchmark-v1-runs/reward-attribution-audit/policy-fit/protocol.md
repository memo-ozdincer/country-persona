# Fit a team to directional rewards that agree across writers

Use one tightly bounded leave-event-out comparison. Three delegates each hold one fixed archived note and simultaneously choose send or silence. Delivery is independently lossy, and the sender pays a fee even if the note is lost. The sender observes the rate, fee and its own standalone credit in the existing verbatim source rubric. Train three residual coefficients shared by all delegates with the same initial policy, costs and update budget. This is an exact team policy-gradient experiment on archives with a centralized training scorer.

Compare two updates. The nominal update uses each actor's conditional effect on the reference writer's final release, net of costs and marginalized over other actors and delivery. The agreement update uses the bound nearest to zero when all archived writers and grade alternatives agree on a strict sign. It skips that actor's term otherwise. Three features describe a common intercept, local credit and delivery rate. The deployable initial policy sends when the expected standalone credit exceeds the fee. At evaluation, use the final greedy residual policy without writer outcomes in its inputs and the same initial policy as a fixed control. Full-information per-writer exact search is an evaluation ceiling only.

Fit on Resolution 2475 and evaluate on 2686, then reverse the split. Preserve all results per held writer. Use the four existing delivery probabilities and modeled send fees of 1, 3, 5 and 8 documentary points, with equally weighted scenes. Report stochastic training score improvements and stable update coverage separately from held greedy scores. One case family supplies each fit, and both are exposed. Historical writer artifacts and the release metric remain fixed. No fresh generation or optimization of an LLM is involved.
