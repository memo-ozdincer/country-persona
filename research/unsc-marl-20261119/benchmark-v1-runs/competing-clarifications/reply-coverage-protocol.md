# Keep both recipient branches in training

In the initial held replay, the learned chair is close to exact
planning with an optimal responder. The learned responder sends on
every reachable negative branch in two-opening worlds even when that
negative cannot complete a certificate. For one matched fit, allocate
half the training probability to the learned chair, a quarter to an
ask to the first role, and a quarter to an ask to the second. Preserve
the initial model, 96 updates, features and reward. Deploy the learned
chair with no forced questions. Check the original sender and chair
swaps and the response errors in the selected branches. Also report
whether the chair changes recipient correctly when the two delivery
rates are exchanged and the exact action switches.
