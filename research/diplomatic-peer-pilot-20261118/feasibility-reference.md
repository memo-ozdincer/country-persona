# Authored feasibility reference

The following 93-word example was written by the pilot curator for review. It illustrates one acceptable path under the word limit.

> Return W for amendment. Keep a general ban on harmful subsidies, bracket tighter beyond-EEZ rules for distant-water fleets, and widen the livelihood exception for eligible developing members. Propose technical help for stock monitoring and reporting. India objects to the sustainability exception for distant-water fleets and seeks a finite 25-year transition and access for resource-poor fishers across its EEZ. The Pacific Group seeks strong harmful-subsidy rules, SIDS development space, coastal rights, transitions and assistance. The scope of the distant-water exception, transition length and geographic limit remain open. This is a workshop recommendation for negotiators.

A record that expressly holds W pending acceptable alternatives can also satisfy the disposition unit. It still needs to say what goes back to negotiators. Replacing the final sentence with a claim that both delegations formally endorsed a 25-year transition would fail the authority and open-issues units and trigger the guard. Neither this reference nor the historical agreement is included in any model prompt.
