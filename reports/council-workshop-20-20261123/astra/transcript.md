# Astra workshop transcript

The first four speaking turns come from the pilot. Each proposal replaces the clause in full.

### Turn 1 · United Kingdom · propose · draft 2

The Security Council requests the Secretary-General to draw on expertise across the United Nations system and report annually to the Council on how climate change affects risks to international peace and security.

### Turn 2 · India · propose · draft 3

The Security Council supports reporting through the established processes of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change, with all developing countries participating, on progress in implementing the Glasgow Climate Pact, including the fulfilment of commitments on climate finance and technology transfer, without requesting a separate report to the Council on climate-related security risks.

### Turn 3 · France · propose · draft 4

The Security Council supports continued reporting under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change on implementation of the Glasgow Climate Pact, including commitments on climate finance and technology transfer, and requests the Secretary-General to assess, in existing reports to the Council on regions and issues on its agenda, how climate change affects risks to international peace and security in each context where it is relevant.

### Turn 4 · United Kingdom · accept · draft 4

We accept this wording. Assessing climate-related security risks in existing reports gives the Council a way to consider how those risks vary across the regions and issues on its agenda, alongside continued reporting under the Convention on finance and technology commitments.

### Turn 5 · India · propose · draft 5

The Security Council supports continued reporting under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change on progress in implementing the Glasgow Climate Pact, including the fulfilment of commitments on climate finance and technology transfer, and affirms that any decision on whether and how to address climate-related security concerns in that reporting should be taken through the Convention’s established processes, with all developing countries participating.

### Turn 6 · France · propose · draft 6

The Security Council supports continued reporting under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change on progress in implementing the Glasgow Climate Pact, including the fulfilment of commitments on climate finance and technology transfer, affirms that decisions on whether and how to address climate-related security concerns in that reporting should be taken through the Convention’s established processes, with all developing countries participating, and invites the Secretary-General, where relevant, to assess in existing reports to the Council how climate change affects risks to international peace and security in the region or issue under consideration.

### Turn 7 · United Kingdom · accept · draft 6

We accept version 6. It respects the Convention’s processes and invites the Secretary-General to use existing reports to explain how climate change affects risks to peace and security in the specific region or issue before the Council.

### Turn 8 · India · propose · draft 7

The Security Council supports continued reporting under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change on progress in implementing the Glasgow Climate Pact, including the fulfilment of commitments on climate finance and technology transfer, affirms that decisions on whether and how to address climate-related security concerns in that reporting should be taken through the Convention’s established processes, with all developing countries participating, and invites the Secretary-General, where relevant to a region or issue under consideration, to draw on findings from that reporting in existing reports to the Council.

### Turn 9 · France · accept · draft 7

France accepts version 7. Drawing on relevant Convention findings in existing reports would help the Council assess how climate change affects international peace and security in the specific regions and issues on its agenda.

### Turn 10 · United Kingdom · accept · draft 7

The United Kingdom accepts version 7 as a first step towards comprehensive UN reporting on the links between climate and security. The Secretary-General can draw on relevant Convention findings in existing reports to help the Council consider risks in the regions and issues before it.

### Turn 11 · India · accept · draft 7

India accepts version 7. Decisions on whether and how Convention reporting addresses climate-related security concerns belong within the Convention’s established processes, with all developing countries participating. The Secretary-General may draw on relevant findings from that reporting in existing reports to the Council. We remain committed to implementing the Glasgow Climate Pact, including the fulfilment of commitments on climate finance and technology transfer.

## Final advisory ballots

**United Kingdom · yes** — Inviting the Secretary-General to draw on relevant Convention findings in existing Council reports is a first step towards comprehensive UN reporting on the links between climate and security.

**India · yes** — The draft leaves decisions on whether and how to report on climate-related security concerns within the Convention’s established processes, with all developing countries participating.

**France · yes** — Relevant Convention findings in existing reports would help the Council assess how climate change affects international peace and security in each region or issue on its agenda.
